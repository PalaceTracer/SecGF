# Expanded song-style catalog

Prepared 16 October 2026 from the supplied catalog and proposed song filter. This is a curated expansion of musical organizations, not an exhaustive taxonomy, a record of confirmed personal favorites, or a measure of Suno capabilities. The attached filter governs this task only. Original sources remain unchanged.

Use these entries as discovery resources, not selectable templates or a fixed rotation. Establish the current song’s needs before consulting examples. The relationships describe possible recordings: sourced identity and musical practice are distinguished from original arrangement proposals in the final column. “Could suit” suggestions are possibilities, not restrictions. No generated audio was heard or tested.

Taxonomy has overlapping boundaries. Some established song forms are grouped as subgenres for navigation and explicitly identified as forms. Texture, performance practice and instrumental roles are labeled arrangement treatments. Original hybrids are proposals, not newly claimed genres. Synthpop/electropop and related umbrella labels overlap; counts are entries, not independent musical territories.

For intended singing, specify a recurring pitched verse contour and develop any actual refrain without inventing one. Preserve finished lyrics. Standing no-rap and intentional section-performance constraints remain in force. Taxonomic geographic names in the first column identify traditions; the copy-ready musical relationships use audible roles rather than geography, artist names or work titles.

The source list contains 251 unique surviving seeds after cleanup, many of them mechanical hybrids or broad descriptors. They remain in CLEANED_SEEDS.txt. This curated table develops selected useful mechanisms and adds researched alternatives; it does not relabel all surviving seeds as established genres. Unresolved and scope-review labels remain outside the finished catalog. Filtering applies to requested current musical components, not to an indiscriminate ancestry ban: liquid drum and bass and Cajun forms are explicitly retained.

**104 entries:** 24 established genres, 35 subgenres, 20 arrangement treatments and 25 proposed hybrids.

Revision: Eurobeat and Eurodance are explicitly included by the current request. Synthpop was already included and remains one entry. Nine additional hybrids develop these families through different rhythm, harmony, form and ensemble relationships. Eurobeat is not rejected because of where it is popular or distributed; current explicit inclusion takes priority over broad reference-file shortcuts. Eurodance is developed here as fully sung material.
