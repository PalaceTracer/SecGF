# Expanded song-style catalog

This is a curated expansion of musical organizations, not an exhaustive taxonomy, a record of confirmed personal favorites, or a measure of Suno capabilities.

Use these entries as discovery resources, not selectable templates or a fixed rotation. Establish the current song’s needs before consulting examples. The relationships describe possible recordings: sourced identity and musical practice are distinguished from original arrangement proposals in the final column. Browse by musical organization and current preferences; table order has no selection priority. No generated audio was heard or tested.

Taxonomy has overlapping boundaries. Some established song forms are grouped as subgenres for navigation and explicitly identified as forms. Texture, performance practice and instrumental roles are labeled arrangement treatments. Original hybrids are proposals, not newly claimed genres. Synthpop/electropop and related umbrella labels overlap; counts are entries, not independent musical territories.

For intended singing, specify a recurring pitched verse contour and develop any actual refrain without inventing one. Preserve finished lyrics. Intentional section-performance constraints remain in force. Taxonomic geographic names in the first column identify traditions; the copy-ready musical relationships use audible roles rather than geography, artist names or work titles.


Categories distinguish established genres, subgenres, arrangement treatments and proposed hybrids. Boundaries may overlap. Liquid drum and bass and Cajun forms are included; exclusions must not be inferred from ancestry.

Eurobeat, Eurodance and synthpop are included. Distribution or popularity does not determine eligibility. Eurodance is developed here as fully sung material.
