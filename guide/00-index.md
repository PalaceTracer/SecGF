# 00 — Operational reading route

This guide designs intended sound from finished lyrics, current intent, confirmed preferences and written evidence. It does not write lyrics or evaluate audio. All wording examples are untested design proposals. The current production contract is exactly 1,000 tool-counted Style characters. This is the user's requirement, not a measured Suno capability.

## Routine route

Read this index, [01 purpose and evidence](01-purpose-interfaces.md), [02 musical organization](02-attribute-vocabulary.md) and [10 compiler](10-style-compiler.md). These contain the required procedures and constraints. The catalog is a separate discovery resource retrieved for an open sound brief when accessible. For sung work use [06 vocals](06-vocal-architecture.md); consult [09 lyric fit](09-lyric-music-fit.md) for phrasing and [11 written audit](11-written-design-audit.md) before returning. Use other craft chapters for consequential design questions; their dimensions are not clauses to fill.

Form a musical direction from the brief and confirmed preferences. For an open sound brief, consult relevant catalog entries during direction formation when accessible, before choosing ensemble, groove or timbre. A specific brief can proceed directly. An unavailable catalog calls for proportionate use of available craft knowledge and honest disclosure when the task depends on the missing source. Defer complete profiles and teaching cases until a specific craft question makes them useful. An explicitly requested reference is current intent and can be researched immediately. Meaningful uncertainty can justify research; it does not require retreat to familiar material. A better musical idea found later can reshape the direction. No bank candidate, example, genre, influence, contrast or novelty receives a guaranteed slot.

## Style discovery inside this folder

[Style catalog](STYLE_CATALOG.md) contains discovery entries with musical relationships, categories, source boundaries and scope exceptions. For an open sound brief, retrieve relevant parts while forming a direction when accessible. Explore by musical organization across relevant parts, including time, pitch, ensemble, vocal behavior, form and image; catalog order does not rank suitability. Do not read every entry or force a catalog candidate; tightly specified briefs can proceed directly. Absence is not exclusion; membership is neither taste nor model support.

Use the craft chapters, current brief, confirmed preferences and available knowledge. Do not assume access to resources absent from the repository or pretend to have read them. Research a consequential uncertainty only when needed and accessible. An unavailable reference does not block a supported original design.

## Delivery rule

Return exactly STYLE OF MUSIC, CHARACTER COUNT, EXCLUDE, RATIONALE SONG TITLE as defined in 10. Preserve finished lyrics; specify recurring pitched verse behavior for intended singing and treat only actual refrains. Special speech/chant/whisper/shout modes need explicit intent or an intentional performance tag. No artist/work/geographic shortcuts or researchIDs in final Style or complete examples. No audio files, invented heard results or listening task.
